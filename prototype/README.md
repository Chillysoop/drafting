# Browser prototype

`green-flag-deck.html` is the playable browser slice, version 46 (Oct 28, 2026). It is one self-contained file: open it in a browser to play. It is the reference for every race rule until the Unity port reaches parity (see `../docs/port-plan.md`).

What v45 has: 20 cars and 10 teams; five tracks (Millbrook short track, Carver intermediate, Talon Ridge superspeedway, Pinewood kart circuit, Ridgeback road course); 12 s draw beats with slow-mo and drag-to-play; five-tier odds on every card; combos, Heat, Momentum and Focus; crew radio calls; real pit road with fuel windows, cautions and the pit stop card game; car setup and tunes that shift the deck; lasting damage; five personas; a season with a calendar (10 races in stock cars, 8 in late models, 6 in karts since v45), points standings and save/continue in browser storage (v36, roadmap step 15); a card collection and a race deck of 12 to 16 cards you build before each race in a deck builder that shows real cards, with track minimums and slot limits set by your tune (v37, step 15b); one HUD console with Focus on top and the combo row under it, toss effects as countdown or READY tiles above it, and a toss bin you drag cards into (v38); a qualifying mini-game before each season race that sets your grid spot: a rolling start, then 2 timed laps where you time your braking at every corner, hold for Focus slow-mo, chain clean corners, and trim the run with one card from your race deck (v39, step 16); sponsors and race objectives: sign one sponsor per season for money every race, a season goal with a bonus and one race objective of their choosing each week; every race has 2 to 3 objectives scaled to your car, and meeting all of them moves your rewards up a tier; at season end the sponsor renews with a raise or walks, and the career carries on into the next season (v40, step 17); a driver sheet and d20 checks off track: pick a background (attributes, two proficient skills, a trait and a flaw), earn XP and Hype from races, and face a press scrum, a sponsor dinner or a contract talk between races, where every answer shows its modifiers by source, the DC, the roll needed and the five-tier odds before the roll and what would have helped after it; answers move your image (Humble/Cocky, Professional/Showman), and results change fans, respect, sponsor money, your team salary, a car or crew clause and your Momentum at the next green flag (v41, step 18); Crew HQ between races: a crew chief, spotter, engineer and pit crew you train, level with XP from the calls you take and the stops you make, give perks at levels 4, 6 and 8, or replace with a hire who brings a trait and a wage; levels 6 and 8 unlock six bigger radio calls (bump and run, three-wide hole, fuel stretch, all in, engine map, setup trick), and the pit crew works your pit cards, which you upgrade (+) or swap for new ones from a dealer (v42, step 19); six named rivals with personalities (the Old Pro, the Enforcer, the Media Darling, the Hothead, the Wall, the Grudge) and a Grudge toward you from Respect to Feud: wrecking one makes them block you, lean on you and cost your passing cards odds next time, and once Hot they can pay you back with a hook; this week's rival sets a rival objective, and rivals message your garage inbox after races, where replies are d20 checks that move the Grudge (v43, step 20); a kart rung (v44, step 21): pick "Start in: Karts" on the title for a kart series on Pinewood Kart Park and the new Hollow Creek Kartway, with 13- to 14-lap sprints, no cautions or pit stops, karts close to spec, four kart personas (Brawler, Line Painter, Pack Rat, Late Braker) and a 13-card kart deck whose combos chain card to card (Show the Inside into Switchback, Around the Outside into Own the Inside on the next corner) inside a shorter window and a x3 cap, Tow pips from running in a train that power Pull Out and Late Brake, partners who push back, and a front fairing that three scruffy pushes put in for a 5 s penalty at the flag; late models and promotion (v45, step 22): new careers start in karts and climb Karts → Late models → Stock cars in one save; late models race stock car cards and combos with pit stops and cautions on Cedar Bowl (a quarter-mile bullring), Millbrook at late model length and Crossroads Figure-8 (twice a lap the crossover puts an Evade card in your hand when a car on the other leg gets there with you), cars closer to spec than stock cars and an 11 to 15 card deck; each series has its own ten teams; at season end contract offers by points position (stay, a better team in the same series, or one to three teams from the next series, top 14 only), each negotiable once with a d20 Negotiate check; a team switch keeps your driver, money, collection and parts and gives you the new team's car and crew, a promotion out of karts turns kart cards into their stock car cousins and grows the persona into its stock car one, and your rivals move up with half their Grudge; season balance (v46, step 23): race pay and sponsor money scale by series (karts 15%, late models 30%, stock cars 40% of the old pay), promotions come from the next series' smaller teams so each rung starts near the back again, qualifying adds the same driver term for every car (it used to count your stats twice), the field races back (a pass reads lower against a car ahead with good Defense, a defend card against a car behind with good Racecraft, and late model and stock car fields are sharper than karts), Grit adds odds in the last third of a race and Wits keeps crew calls up longer, and driver coaching follows you when you change teams while the car and crew stay with the team; plus rewards and a shop; test track practice mode; the first-lap tutorial; and four looks to compare (press L).

## Changing it

Edit the HTML directly and open a pull request. Before merging a rule or balance change:

1. `node harness/smoke.js "$PWD/green-flag-deck.html"` and `node harness/season-smoke.js "$PWD/green-flag-deck.html"` and `node harness/deck-smoke.js "$PWD/green-flag-deck.html"` and `node harness/hud-smoke.js "$PWD/green-flag-deck.html"` and `node harness/quali-smoke.js "$PWD/green-flag-deck.html"` and `node harness/sponsor-smoke.js "$PWD/green-flag-deck.html"` and `node harness/driver-smoke.js "$PWD/green-flag-deck.html"` and `node harness/crew-smoke.js "$PWD/green-flag-deck.html"` and `node harness/rival-smoke.js "$PWD/green-flag-deck.html"` and `node harness/kart-smoke.js "$PWD/green-flag-deck.html"` and `node harness/promo-smoke.js "$PWD/green-flag-deck.html"` and `node harness/season-bal-smoke.js "$PWD/green-flag-deck.html"` must pass with 0 page errors.
2. Run the harness on the same seed before and after, and put both summaries in the PR:
   `cd harness && bash rp.sh "$PWD/../green-flag-deck.html" after 40 none,human,spam ',"seed":61'` then `python3 sum6.py after`.

The page exposes `window.__gfd` (sim functions and state) and honours `window.__gfdHeadless` so the harness can run races without drawing.

## Harness files

- `career-sim.js N html out.json runs [extraJSON]`: step 23. Plays N whole seeded careers per run from a kart season up the ladder with the real between-race rules (sponsor, off-track events as a bot, reward cards and parts, an auto-built race deck, qualifying, garage spending, contract offers). A run is `policy:build`: policy `none`, `human` or `spam`; build `mix`, `driver` (coaching only), `crew` (crew training only), `car` or `save`. Extra: `races` (default 34), `seed`, `start` (`stock`/`late` starts there), `stats`, `econ`, `bal`, `pre` (JS run in the page first), `callMin`, `noStrat`. `crun.sh html tag N runs extra` runs one process per run, and `python3 csum.py dir tag` summarizes (finish by career race block, by series, the first season in each series race by race, season ends and signings, stats and money).
- `career-ui-bot.js html outDir [maxRaces] [speed] [persona]`: step 24. Clicks through a fresh career on the real screens like an attentive player (sponsor, events, garage buys, qualifying with Space taps, races, rewards, offers) and logs screenshots plus UI issues: text off-screen, 'undefined' or 'NaN', page errors, a screen it cannot leave. About an hour for karts to stock cars at 4x; not in CI.
- `season-bal-smoke.js`: step 23 rules: pay and sponsor scale by series, promotion offers and the sponsor rescale on a promotion, the field and Defense tags on a stock car card, Grit late in the race, Wits on a crew call, the driver sheet's race-day panel, the garage coaching note and the kart pit crew lock.
- `sim-harness-v9.js`: v8 plus `sched` (pick the season calendar, e.g. `"kart"`, before the persona so kart personas and the kart deck are used). Output adds `pairs` (card to card combo links), `fair`, `fout`, `flost` (fairing hits, penalties and places lost), `rep` (Late Brake repasses) and `push` (partner push-backs). Stock car runs match v8 on the same seed.
- `rpk.sh`: the four kart personas over both kart tracks with v9 (`bash rpk.sh html tag N policies extra`).
- `rpl.sh`: the five stock personas over the three late model tracks with v9 and `sched` `"late"` (`bash rpl.sh html tag N policies extra`).
- `sim-harness-v8.js`: v7 plus `crew` (a build for the game's `crewSetup()`: `stats`, `perks`, `traits`, `pitUp` pit card upgrades), `stats` (set) and `statAdd` (add) for any stat, and `callMin` (lowest call chance the bots accept, default 55). Output adds `calls` and `cwon`. With a default crew, seeded runs match v7 for personas without a crew bonus.
- `sim-harness-v7.js`: runs N headless races per persona and track for each play style (`none` hands-off, `smart`, `spam`, `human`, `human2`, `bold`, `hspam`). `seed` makes runs repeatable. `coll` adds cards to the collection, and `deckFor` races a deck auto-built for `"same"` track or for another track (a number = track offset). `quali` (`skip`, `poor`, `avg`, `good`, `great`) plays qualifying as a bot before each race, and `qcard: "best"` lets it pick a qualifying card; leave them out for the old roll-off (seeded runs unchanged).
- `rp.sh`: one process per persona over all five tracks.
- `sum6.py`, `pp.py`, `tsum.py`: summaries (finish vs expected by play style, persona x track table, tune sweeps).
- `smoke.js`: loads the page and runs one race per track.
- `deck-smoke.js`: drives the deck builder (clear, auto-build, tune changes the slot limits and Aero the deck size, add past a limit is blocked, fix rules), checks the car sheet shows the race deck as cards, checks that the garage fixes a deck that breaks the next track's minimum, that a reward card lands in the collection, and that saves from before the collection still load.
- `season-smoke.js`: plays a 10-race season through the real screens (races fast-forwarded), reloads the page mid-season and mid-race to check save/continue, and checks that a race cut off mid-way starts over on Ironman too (board Q48: no). Since step 17 it signs a sponsor first, checks the reward objectives and the sponsor review at season end, and starts season 2. Pass a folder as the second argument to save screenshots.
- `quali-smoke.js`: step 16 qualifying through the real screens: picks a card, plays the run with real Space key events (and one hold for Focus slow-mo), checks the grid, that you start where you qualified with the card in the discard pile, that a reload mid-race keeps the grid, the BRAKE button hold, Give up, and the phone layout.
- `sponsor-smoke.js`: step 17 sponsors and objectives: Go racing waits for a contract, season-1 offers leave out the sponsors that need a good season, all objectives met moves rewards up a tier (a DNF never does), season goal states, season end renews (bonus paid, +20% offer) or walks (not offered again), saves from before step 17 still load, and the phone layout of the offers.
- `crew-smoke.js`: step 19 Crew HQ: the garage links to Crew HQ (crew training left the shop), four staff with perk tiers, training at the listed price, two-tap perk lock-in only from its level, level 6 and 8 calls never offered below their level and offered above it, a forced call's effect and XP, an XP level-up at the finish, a hire's trait and wage (taken off the payout), pit card upgrade and dealer swap, pit crew perks reaching the stop (Deep bench draws 6), saves from before step 19, and the phone layout. The season smoke now buys Racecraft in the shop.
- `kart-smoke.js`: step 21 kart rung: the title's Karts pick (kart personas, kart calendar), a kart career's deck, series and its 10 to 13 card limits (the tune can shift the top by one), the real garage and qualifying screens into a kart race, the Kart module in place of the pit module, PIT and wrecks bring no stop or caution, Show the Inside marking a covering kart and Switchback's +20, Own the Inside linking only on an opposite-direction corner, the shorter window and x3 cap, Tow pips and Pull Out, fairing in and the penalty reorder at the flag and on the reward screen, kart-only rewards and relics, the save keeping the kart series, a stock career never seeing kart cards, and the phone layout.
- `promo-smoke.js`: step 22 ladder: karts are the default title pick, the Late models pick (stock personas, late calendar), a kart season ending P2 brings stay, better-team and three late model offers, one d20 negotiation per offer, signing a late model offer (series, deck limits, translated cards, persona, the new team's car and crew, driver and money kept, rivals with half their Grudge), the save keeping it, a late model race on Crossroads Figure-8 through the real screens, a late season ending P6 bringing two stock car offers and signing one, a P17 finish bringing only a stay offer, and the phone layout.
- `rival-smoke.js`: step 20 rivals: six rivals in fixed seats with their traits and no Grudge, the garage rivals list, inbox and rival sheet, a rival objective naming this week's rival (the same driver the race tags), a wreck adding Grudge and leaving a message and a reward row, the harder rival objective next race, a d20 reply moving the Grudge, passing and defending odds, blocking, attack lean and payback chance by Grudge, a real AI move turning into payback at Grudge 8 (never at 0), saves from before step 20, and the phone layout. The sponsor smoke now expects the extra rival objective.
- `rival-season.js N html [policies] [off]`: whole seeded seasons in one career per policy, with Grudge carried race to race: average finish, DNFs, rival wrecks and paybacks per season, and how often a rival is Warm or Hot. `off` switches the rival effects off on the same seeds.
- `driver-smoke.js`: step 18 driver sheet and off-track events: title background, race XP and Hype, the garage holds Go racing until the event is done or skipped, every answer in all three events shows labeled modifiers, DC, roll needed and odds that match a brute-force count, every result shows what would have helped (forced misses), Hype advantage and reroll, contract salary paid next race, banked Momentum at the green flag, image signature answers, spending an attribute point, Feel hides the odds, saves from before step 18 still load, and the phone layout. Since step 18 the season and sponsor smokes skip the off-track event.
- `quali-sim.js`: average starting spot by qualifying skill per track (`node quali-sim.js "$PWD/../green-flag-deck.html" 300 [statBoost]`). Done-when for step 16: `good` starts 4+ places ahead of `skip`.
